## Frozen research outputs

Machine-readable derived outputs are provided for scientific transparency and reproducibility of the associated study. Reuse should preserve appropriate attribution to the associated manuscript/repository and should not be interpreted as transferring ownership of upstream benchmark data.

## Manuscript files

The public repository/release intentionally excludes the submitted manuscript source/PDF, cover letter and other private journal-submission files during peer review. Their absence is a deliberate publication-workflow choice rather than a reproducibility gap in the public computational evidence.

## Citation

Please use `CITATION.cff` for citation metadata. The frozen computational submission state is preserved in release `v1.0.0-submission`; publication DOI metadata can be added after the article is published.
